title: Inspecting buildings
description: The quick card, the building drawer, and the interactive metrics charts.
---

Click any building to select it. A quick card shows its name, live status and a sparkline. Use it to open the **building drawer**, which has two tabs:

## Overview

Current status, last deploy, and live readings.

## Metrics

Interactive charts for **CPU, memory, network and disk**, over a time range you choose — 1 h / 6 h / 24 h / 7 d / 30 d — with a **live** mode. Zoom and pan to inspect.

A practical rhythm: short range + live mode for incident triage, long range to spot trends.

## Where the rules live

Monitoring rules are managed org-wide, not per building: open **⚙️ Workflows** in the top bar. See [workflows](/docs/workflows).
