## Seasons

In builder mode (left rail), switch the whole city's palette between **Default / Summer / Fall / Winter** (🌿 / ☀️ / 🍂 / ❄️). The change is instant — every building and tile re-skins at once.

## Ambiance toggles

Next to the season picker, toggle individual effects:

- wind
- water animation
- day/night cycle
- particles
- clouds
- **traffic** (🐴 / 🚀) — the [metric-driven vehicles](/docs/reading-the-city#traffic--network-activity)
- **RAM / disk props** (📦) — the [crates and depots](/docs/reading-the-city#crates--memory-depot--disk) around buildings

Both the season and the ambiance toggles are **saved per organisation**, so each city reopens exactly as you left it.

## Scene templates

Save a city layout and reuse it elsewhere from the **🗺️ Templates** button in the top bar:

- **Save current scene** — captures the landscape (tiles, decorations, decorative structures). It does **not** capture your app/add-on/monitor buildings, which are specific to each org. Choose **Private** or **Public**.
- **Apply** a template (yours or a public one) to the current organisation. Applying replaces the scene; you then re-place your resources.
- **Share** — flip a template to Public and any other user can browse and apply it from the Public tab, with your name shown as the author.
- **Built-in starters** — the Public tab always offers **"Starter — Medieval realm"** and **"Starter — Space outpost"**, the landscapes new maps begin with, so you can return to them anytime (each applies only to a map of its style).
