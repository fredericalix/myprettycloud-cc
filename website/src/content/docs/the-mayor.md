title: The mayor

The mayor is your in-city advisor. They surface recent alerts and recommendations in a popup, so you get a friendly summary of what needs attention without leaving the city view. (In the space theme, the mayor becomes the **Commandant**.)

## The stocktake on city load

When you open a city, the buildings appear at once but take a moment to show their real status colour and live readings while data is gathered. During that window the mayor pins a **"The realm takes stock…"** notice with a live count — *"N of TOTAL holdings have reported."* — and keeps it up until every building is reporting correctly.

Then one of two things happens:

- **All clear** — *"The realm stands true."*
- **Trouble** — the mayor flags any app that has fallen.

This usually takes only a few seconds.

## Muting

The loading notice stays visible even if you've muted the mayor — it's a status indicator, not an alert. The all-clear and trouble messages do respect muting.
