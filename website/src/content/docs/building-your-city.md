title: Building your city

A fresh map doesn't start empty: it opens on a hand-composed **starter scene**. Medieval maps get the **starter realm** — northern mountains, a river bridged by the main road, a sea coast, forests and farms. Space maps get the **starter outpost** — mesas with a quarry and drill, a tunnel spine, landing pads with a resting lander, greenhouse farms, solar panels and wind turbines. In both, the central plaza is left free for your buildings. **Clear** (left rail) resets back to the kit's starter scene, and both are available as [built-in templates](/docs/customization#scene-templates).

Switch to builder mode (left rail) to lay out your town. Select a hex to open the **Inspector** on the left; its top (asset preview + cell coordinates) stays pinned while the rest scrolls.

## Placing your resources

The **Resources** panel on the right lists your apps and add-ons. Click one to drop it on the map, or use **Auto-place** to position them all at once. Auto-placed buildings settle on the village plaza first — placement never builds over roads, rivers, coasts or decorated cells. They fill the buildable ground you already have, and only when the city runs out of room does the map grow just enough new ground at the edge, so buildings never land detached out in the void. Each building is bound to one Clever Cloud resource and coloured by its [live status](/docs/reading-the-city).

## Shaping the landscape

- **Ground tiles** — grass, water, coast, rivers (including dry "waterless" variants), roads and slope ramps; rotate any tile in 60° steps. Lay roads between buildings and the city's [traffic](/docs/reading-the-city#traffic--network-activity) will use them.
- **Elevation** — raise or lower a cell in steps (up to 4) to sculpt plains, hills and plateaus. Raised tiles get a solid cliff skirt so nothing floats.
- **Structures** — walls, fences, gates, bridges, a wheat field, dirt or ruins; rotate them to align.
- **Decorations** — trees, hills, mountains, rocks, water plants and props, grouped in the palette. Each placed decoration can be rotated individually.

## Working with placed buildings

- **Move** — select a placed building and click **Move** (or press **M**) to relocate it to another tile; **Esc** cancels.
- **Hover** any building to see its name in a small floating label (works in builder and view mode).

## Autosave

Your layout **saves automatically** per organisation — there is no save button, and the city reopens exactly as you left it.

To reuse a landscape elsewhere or restyle the whole city, see [seasons, ambiance & templates](/docs/customization).
